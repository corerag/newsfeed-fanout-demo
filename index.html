FOLLOWER FEED — READ-TIME MERGE

  • Select a follower and view their feed to see push and pull items merge by time.
Modeled on the news feed system in the System Design Primer: fan-out-on-write trades expensive writes for instant reads, fan-out-on-read trades a merge-time cost for cheap writes on high-fan-out accounts, a message queue smooths the write burst so producers never block on it, and sharding (here, follower_id % shards) spreads that write load across stores. Most real feeds use a hybrid, exactly like the merge below. The queue is also bounded: past capacity it applies backpressure — new jobs are dropped (load shedding) and the fan-out service throttles until the queue drains, rather than growing without limit. A rate limiter sits even earlier, in front of both lanes: a fixed per-account token bucket that rejects excess posts outright, checked before any fan-out or queue work starts — a proactive client-side quota, independent of how loaded the system happens to be.
